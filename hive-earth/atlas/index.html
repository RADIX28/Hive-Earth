Tap the corners of your field on the map, then close the shape. The atlas measures the area, reads the ground level across it, resolves it to a district and basin, pulls five years of hourly reanalysis, and reports what that ground has done — not what it is doing.

Where the data is coarser than your land, it says so instead of guessing.
